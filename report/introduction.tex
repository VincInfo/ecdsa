\section{Introduction}

In the digital era, securing electronic transactions and communications is crucial. Digital signatures play an essential role in ensuring the authenticity and integrity of data, providing a secure means of verifying identities in a virtual environment. Among the various algorithms that facilitate digital signatures, the Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA) stands out due to its efficiency and robust security features. This seminar will explore ECDSA's critical role, its operational mechanisms, and its applications while highlighting its comparative advantages over traditional algorithms like RSA and DSA.

ECDSA is particularly valued in scenarios requiring high security with operational efficiency, such as mobile device authentication, secure email, and blockchain transactions. Its capability to maintain equivalent security levels to RSA and DSA at significantly reduced key sizes not only minimizes computational load but also enhances transaction speeds, making it ideal for modern applications.

This introduction will delve into the mathematical foundations of ECDSA, rooted in elliptic curve cryptography, which relies on the complexity of the Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithm Problem (ECDLP) to deter attackers. The seminar will also address potential security risks and the implications of quantum computing on the future resilience of ECDSA.

By providing a comprehensive overview of ECDSA within the broader context of digital signature technology, this seminar aims to illuminate its significance in securing digital identities and transactions, ensuring participants understand both its practical applications and theoretical underpinnings.
